I’m Dr. Carlo De la Sancha, a pathologist. My job is to look under the microscope at the parts of your body almost nobody gets to see, and to figure out why they get sick.

Solving a medical mystery almost never comes down to the symptom a patient walks in with. You have to step back and see the body for what it is: a machine of connected parts, where a problem in one place can set off an alarm somewhere completely different. That’s where the right diagnosis is found.

Every two weeks, I’ll take you with me through a medical case: a symptom that didn’t add up, and how the body, seen as a whole, told us its story from the inside. What was going on, why, and what you can do to take care of yourself.

And we’ll build this together: reply to any of my emails and tell me what part of your body you’d like to understand. An organ, a disease, something you’ve always been curious about. That’s where the next cases come from. I don’t give medical consultations here, but if you tell me your question, I’ll turn it into a case that helps all of us.
